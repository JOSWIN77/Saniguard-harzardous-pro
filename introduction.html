Vision & Architecture

Rethinking Industrial Headwear for the Connected Era.

Hard hats have remained passive plastic shells for over a century. SaniGuard embeds intelligent telemetry directly into the headwear to eliminate workplace blind spots.

A Century of Evolution

From steamed canvas to on-edge intelligence.

1919

Hard-Boiled Canvas

Steamed canvas dipped in black resin for California gold miners. Protected only against small direct rocks.

1950s

Thermoplastic Caps

Injection-molded polyethylene shells. Lightweight and non-conductive, but completely blind to toxic air and worker vitals.

2015

1st-Gen Smart Caps

Short-range Bluetooth beepers with single gas sensors. Zero communication coverage in subterranean environments.

2026 // SANIGUARD

Edge-AI Mesh Sentinel

Quad-gas chamber, 6-axis IMU, subterranean LoRa mesh, OLED HUD, and on-edge AI life-support system.

Three Core Pillars

Autonomous protection operating at every level.

01

Sense

Continuous 100ms multi-gas aspiration (Methane, H₂S, CO, O₂), 6-axis impact motion, and forehead infrared thermometry.

02

Decide

0.2-second on-edge neural inference evaluates gas plume slopes and worker physiological strain before human senses can react.

03

Connect

Subterranean LoRaWAN peer-to-peer mesh ensures continuous data relay across deep mines, steel vessels, and tunnels without cell service.
